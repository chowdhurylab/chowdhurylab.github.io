# CatLog release October 12, 2026

- Records: 168,401, of them 45,545 accepted (Verified and Corrected). Data ID 94c19d6af4.
- New from CatLog: CatLog read 1,232 papers; 1,216 of them give 31,790 records and 16 give none.
  Each value was read from the paper by an AI model and checked against the paper by a second, separate AI run;
  code converted the units. These records replace the papers' 22,835 older records (3,211 of them accepted); papers that give none no longer show older records.
- Older records: the October 7, 2026 catalog (data generation d241dad83bc1).
- Site changes in this release: The kcat/Km warning explains itself (the printed kcat/Km, kcat ÷ Km, how far apart they are and the likely cause), and a record's values in the paper are one table: value, as printed, as stored, and the second check; The Guide says what the CatRange paper is: it describes CatLog-27k, 27,176 kinetic records curated from BRENDA and SABIO-RK on which its models were trained, and this site is the growing catalog that followed; the Paper tab and the citation link name the CatRange paper; Guide matches the new table and record panel: search takes a PMID or DOI, the table sorts both ways and has T (°C) and pH columns, an open record's address is a link to it, and the worked example shows the record's values table (printed in paper, stored as, second check); a kcat/Km flag gives both numbers, how far apart they are and the likely cause; Plainer Guide and Stats wording: shorter status meanings without repeats or dates, one note per line under Field meanings, the license note no longer repeats the source list, the Stats group meanings are a short list, and Guide style rules that no longer applied are removed; Record panel in one clean order: one status line, each warning said once under the values with a "!" button that jumps to it, the paper's values as one table in the Paper section, older review notes on request, and one licence line; Record panel: a record without a linked paper no longer says "Accepted" twice, a sequence source that only repeats the UniProt line is left out, and the stylesheet stays under 64 KB; Record panel: on a phone the status line wraps after a separator, never before one, and the kcat/Km unit note points to the source's list only when it is shown; Temperature and pH are two plain number columns again, T (°C) and pH, and the room left on wide screens goes to the Enzyme, Organism and Substrate columns (first until their names fit on one line), so the table fills the window and the arrow sits right after Status; kcat, Km, kcat/Km, temperature and pH sort both ways: the Sort menu lists high to low and low to high, a click on the column header sorts high to low and a second click low to high, and an arrow in the header shows the direction; records without the value stay last; Phone cards show the EC number at the end of the name line, and a caution beside a value shows its short words (check units, values differ, above limit) instead of relying on a tooltip a touch screen cannot show; Each open record has its own link: the address ends in #record= and the record's ID while it is open, and opening such a link shows that record on its page of the table; a link to a record this snapshot does not have says so; On phones the Download page button reads Download, so the Sort menu has room to show its choice, and the temperature sorts are named after the T (°C) column; The search box finds a paper's records by PMID or DOI (in full, by its beginning, or as a doi.org link): the table index now carries each record's PMIDs and DOIs, taken from the Full data download of the same snapshot when the index is built; In a narrow window names wrap between words before a word is broken, EC numbers never wrap, and the Download page button keeps a normal space between its words.
- Human review: 1,992 records had every value approved by a human reviewer; the 1,795 accepted ones show "human-checked".

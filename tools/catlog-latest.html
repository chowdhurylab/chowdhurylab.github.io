What one row includes

Full data includes sequences and SMILES where available. Review files add source records and review notes.

Field meanings

kcat
Turnover number (s-1)
Km
Substrate concentration at half-maximal rate (mM)
kcat/Km
Catalytic efficiency (s-1 mM-1). Calculated values are marked.
T and pH
Temperature and pH recorded for the measurement.

A dash means a value is missing or hidden after a data check. Open the row to see why.

Unit not recorded means the source did not give a unit; the column unit does not apply. A warning marks a value to check against its source. Open the row for the reason.

For a variant, the wild-type sequence is the reference. A variant sequence is shown only when it was supplied or reconstructed.

Review status

Accepted
CatLog checked the kinetic value and enzyme identity. The value was kept as reported or updated from a saved source.
Accepted (identity only)
The protein identity and sequence were checked, but CatLog has no paper reference for the kinetic value.
Further checks
Review is unfinished. The sequence, substrate structure, or match to the original measurement still needs checking.
Unreviewed or excluded
The row is unreviewed, was calculated from other reported values, or remains disputed.
Paper evidence
A value and its table or measurement excerpt are saved from the paper. This alone does not mean Accepted.
Source note
A database note is saved; no paper-value excerpt is attached.

CatLog uses automated checks. A lab member reviews cases that need a decision.

Status describes our review, not the paper's quality.

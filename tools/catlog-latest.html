Using CatLog

Search, open a row, and download.

Public data and review files

Public data can be downloaded now. The lab can also share paper excerpts and review notes.

Values

kcat
Reactions per enzyme per second (s-1).
Km
Concentration at half the maximum rate (mM).
kcat/Km
Catalytic efficiency (s-1 mM-1). Calculated values are marked.
T and pH
Measurement conditions.
Sequence
Wild type is the reference. Variants appear only when the mutation is clear.

A dash means missing. Unit not recorded means CatLog did not guess. A warning marks a value to check.

Review labels

Accepted
Value and enzyme match checked; the value may be original or corrected.
Accepted (identity only)
Protein checked; no paper link for the kinetic value.
Further checks
Sequence, structure, or source link still needs checking.
Unreviewed or excluded
Not reviewed, calculated from other values, or disputed.
Saved source text
A paper excerpt or database note is linked to the row. This alone does not mean Accepted.

Labels describe CatLog's review, not the paper.

What one row includes

Full data includes sequences and SMILES where available. Review files add source records and review notes.

Field meanings

kcat
Turnover number (s-1)
Km
Substrate concentration at half-maximal rate (mM)
kcat/Km
Catalytic efficiency (s-1 mM-1). Calculated values are marked.
T and pH
Temperature and pH recorded for the measurement.

A dash means a value is missing or hidden after a data check. Open the row to see why.

Unit not recorded means no unit is recorded here; the column unit does not apply. A warning marks a value to check against its source. Open the row for the reason.

For a variant, the wild-type sequence is the reference. A variant sequence is shown only when it was supplied or reconstructed.

Review status

Accepted
Verified and Corrected records: accepted as reported or after correction.
Accepted (identity only)
Accepted on identity checks only, without establishing literature verification of the kinetic value.
Checks pending
Additional checks are required. This is not a rejection.
Other records
Unverified, mathematically inferred, and disputed records are listed separately in Stats.
Paper excerpt
A value and its table or measurement excerpt are saved from the paper. This alone does not mean Accepted.
Database note
A database note is saved; no paper-value excerpt is attached.

CatLog uses automated checks. A saved record is not automatically accepted.

Current literature checks cover the reported value and units, assay context, and protein or variant identity. Missing sequences or unresolved source differences can require follow-up.

Status describes our review, not the paper's quality.

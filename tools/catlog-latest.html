Public data and research records

The public download includes every CatLog row and its curation status. Full research records add the source material and review history behind those fields. Some details are unavailable when they were not reported by the database or paper.

Download now

Published CatLog fields

  • Displayed kinetic values, standardized units, temperature, and pH
  • Enzyme, substrate, organism, and EC number
  • Available protein sequences, variants, and substrate SMILES
  • References, source database, protein match, and curation status
Available by request

How each row was checked

  • The database row or rows retained for review
  • Paper table, row, column, and captured text when available
  • Reported value and unit beside CatLog's standardized value
  • Fields filled or changed, decision notes, and check history

Field meanings

kcat
Turnover rate (s-1)
Km
Substrate concentration at half-maximal rate (mM)
kcat/Km
Catalytic efficiency (s-1 mM-1)
T and pH
Temperature and pH reported for the assay

A dash means that the field is not available for that row.

Curation status

Accepted
Required checks passed. The row may be accepted as reported or after a documented change.
Pending curation
At least one check remains: source, value and unit, protein or substrate identity, or assay conditions.
Provisional
Not in the accepted set. This includes rows not fully assessed, values calculated from other fields, and unresolved disagreements.

These labels describe CatLog's review state, not the quality of the source paper.

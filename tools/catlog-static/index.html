What one row includes

Full data gives the catalog row. Review files add the paper excerpt and review notes.

Field meanings

kcat
Turnover number (s-1)
Km
Substrate concentration at half-maximal rate (mM)
kcat/Km
Catalytic efficiency (s-1 mM-1). Calculated values are marked.
T and pH
Temperature and pH stored for the assay. Values flagged by automated checks are withheld.

A dash means that the field is unavailable or cannot be shown safely in the table. Open the row to see why a temperature or pH value was withheld.

A value marked unit not recorded came without a unit from its source; the column unit does not apply to it. A small warning beside a value means an automated check questioned it, for example a kcat/Km that does not match kcat ÷ Km; open the row for the reason.

For a variant, the wild-type sequence is the reference. A variant sequence is shown only when it was supplied or reconstructed.

Review status

Accepted
CatLog checked the kinetic value and enzyme identity. The value was kept as reported or updated from a saved source.
Accepted (identity only)
The protein identity and sequence were checked, but CatLog has no paper reference for the kinetic value.
Further checks
Review began, but a required check remains open, commonly the sequence, substrate structure, or source match.
Unreviewed or excluded
The row is unreviewed, was calculated from other reported values, or remains disputed.

Automated checks compare source records, papers, and identity databases. A lab member resolves cases that still need a decision.

Saved source text means a paper passage, table reference, or source-database note is attached. It is evidence to inspect, not an acceptance label.

Status describes our review, not the paper's quality.

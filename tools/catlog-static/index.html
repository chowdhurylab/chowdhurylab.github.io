Field meanings

kcat
Turnover rate (s-1)
Km
Substrate concentration at half-maximal rate (mM)
kcat/Km
Catalytic efficiency (s-1 mM-1)
T and pH
Reported assay conditions

A dash means that no displayable value is available for that field.

Curation status

Accepted
Required record checks passed, as reported or after a documented correction.
Pending curation
A source, value and unit, identity, or assay-context check remains open.
Provisional
Outside the accepted set, including not fully assessed, calculated-only, or disputed records.

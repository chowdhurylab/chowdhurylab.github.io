Field meanings

kcat
Turnover rate (s-1)
Km
Substrate concentration at half-maximal rate (mM)
kcat/Km
Catalytic efficiency (s-1 mM-1)
T and pH
Temperature and pH reported for the assay

A dash means that the field is not available for that row.

Curation status

Accepted
Checks are complete. This includes rows kept as reported and rows with documented corrections.
Pending curation
A source, value and unit, identity, or assay check is still open.
Provisional
Not accepted. The row is unreviewed, calculated from other fields, or disputed.

Status describes our review, not the paper's quality.

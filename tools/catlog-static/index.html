Using CatLog

    Search

What each download includes

The public file has the fields most people need. Full review records also show where CatLog found a value and what was checked.

How to read the columns

kcat
Reactions per enzyme per second (s-1).
Km
Substrate concentration at half the maximum rate (mM).
kcat/Km
Catalytic efficiency (s-1 mM-1). Calculated values are marked.
T and pH
Temperature and pH for the measurement. A warning marks an unusual value.

A dash means CatLog has no usable value for that column. Open the row to see what the original source contains.

Unit not recorded means the original database gave no unit, so CatLog does not guess. A warning marks something to check, such as a reported kcat/Km that does not equal kcat ÷ Km.

The wild-type sequence is the reference protein. CatLog shows a variant sequence only when the source states the sequence or mutation clearly.

How review labels work

Accepted
CatLog checked the kinetic value and matched it to the enzyme. The displayed value is either the original or a correction backed by a saved paper, table, or database record.
Accepted (identity only)
CatLog checked the protein identity and sequence, but does not have a paper link for the kinetic value.
Further checks
At least one check is unfinished, usually the sequence, substrate structure, or paper or database entry for the value.
Unreviewed or excluded
The row is unreviewed, was calculated from other reported values, or remains disputed.

Automated checks compare database rows with papers and protein or chemical databases. A lab member reviews cases where the evidence is unclear.

Saved source text is the paper passage, table reference, or database note linked to a row. It helps explain a decision; it does not mean the row was accepted.

The label reports what CatLog has checked. It is not a judgment of the paper's quality.

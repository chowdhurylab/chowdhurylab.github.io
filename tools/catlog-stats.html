Using CatLog

CatLog lists enzyme kinetic constants (kcat, Km, kcat/Km), each with its enzyme, organism, substrate, assay conditions and source paper. It is maintained by the Chowdhury Lab at Iowa State University.

The CatRange paper (PNAS Nexus, 2026) describes CatLog-27k, 27,176 kinetic records curated from BRENDA and SABIO-RK on which its models were trained; this site is the growing catalog that followed.

How this catalog is built: see How CatLog reads and checks values.

How CatLog reads and checks values

Read from the paper
CatLog reads papers directly. An AI model (Claude, Anthropic) reads each kcat, Km and kcat/Km with its unit, enzyme, organism, substrate and assay conditions. A second, separate AI check compares every value with the paper. Code confirms that the quoted line is in the paper text, and converts units where the paper’s differ from the catalog’s. Each value keeps where it is printed and the number as printed. A person has not checked these values unless the record is marked human-checked.
AI audits
A separate AI auditor re-checks sampled values against their papers and tries to find errors; values found wrong are removed.
Human review
Human reviewers compared values with their papers in a 2026 review of the earlier catalog. Accepted records where a reviewer approved every value carry human-checked.
Earlier catalog
The other records come from BRENDA, SABIO-RK, the Open Enzyme Database, UniProt and SKiD, screened by an earlier automated pipeline of AI models and code checks. When CatLog reads a paper, its records replace that paper’s earlier ones.

Find records

Type an enzyme, EC number, organism, substrate, UniProt accession, PMID or DOI in the search box: laccase, 1.10.3.2, P11509, 12514023. Several words narrow the search (Trametes laccase); common names such as human, mouse, yeast or E. coli find the species, and β-lactamase finds beta-lactamase. Gene names are not indexed.

Filters
Source (read from the paper by CatLog or from enzyme databases); review result, with human-checked records; enzyme, organism, substrate and EC number (1.1.1 lists the whole subclass); measurement type (kcat, Km, kcat/Km); wild type or variant; temperature and pH range; cautions.
Sort
By review result (the default), EC number, name or value. A click on a value’s column header sorts high to low, a second click low to high; records without the value stay last.
Share
A filtered view has its own address: copy it from the address bar. So does an open record: the address adds record= and the record ID to the filters.

Example views

  • Human-checked records #browse?checked=1
  • EC 1.1.1, wild type, measured at 25–37 °C #browse?ec=1.1.1&variant=wild&tmin=25&tmax=37

Read a record

Click a row, or press Enter on it, to open the record.

Status line
The review result and its tags, for example Accepted · from paper · human-checked.
Measurement
The values in catalog units, each caution once in words, then substrate, cosubstrate, temperature, pH and the other assay conditions.
Protein and substrate
Wild type or variant, UniProt accession, protein sequence and substrate SMILES, where CatLog has them.
Paper
PubMed and DOI links and the review outcome. A from-paper record lists each value as printed in the paper and as stored. Human review says what a human reviewer approved.
Source details
Where the enzyme name comes from, the source database, the license and the CatLog record ID.

Cautions

An amber beside a value marks one of the first four cautions below; in the record it moves to a note that says what. On phones its short words stand beside it. A missing unit shows as the words no unit after the value, and a temperature to check as a dash in the T column; the record explains both.

values differ
The kcat/Km printed in the paper (or listed by the source) and kcat ÷ Km differ. The record gives both numbers, how far apart they are and, for a familiar factor such as 60 or 1000, the likely cause. All three values are kept.
check units
Km above 10 M or kcat above 107 s-1: the unit or the magnitude may be off.
unit mismatch
The stored kcat/Km may be off by a unit factor such as 1000; where the source lists a kcat/Km that agrees with kcat ÷ Km, the record shows that one.
above limit
kcat/Km is above the diffusion limit: check the units.
no unit
The source gives no unit, so the column unit does not apply.
Temperature
The stored temperature or its unit needs checking (for example a value of exactly 0 °C); the record’s note says which.

The Cautions filter groups them as kcat/Km values differ, Unit or magnitude to check (check units, unit mismatch, above limit), Unit not recorded (no unit) and Temperature to check.

Review result

Each record has one review result and may carry tags.

Accepted
Passed review: read from the paper by CatLog, or accepted by the earlier automated review as printed or after a correction.
Not confirmed
The earlier automated review left a question open on the value, unit, protein or substrate. The record is replaced when CatLog reads its paper.
Not checked
No review result. Unverified: the earlier review recorded no final check. Not reviewed: a database record without a PMID or DOI (SKiD or BRENDA) that was never reviewed. The record shows which, for example Not checked (Unverified).
Disputed
A check found a problem; not accepted.

Tags

from paper
Read from the paper by CatLog (see How CatLog reads and checks values).
corrected
Accepted after a correction in the earlier automated review; the value before the correction was not kept.
no paper linked
Accepted, but no PMID or DOI is linked, so the value cannot be checked against a paper.
human-checked
A human reviewer approved every value on the record (on a record read by CatLog: the same values in the earlier catalog). Shown on accepted records only; most records have not been checked by a person.
Evidence labels on older records
Located in the paper
The earlier automated review saved where the paper prints the values; the text may describe context rather than this value. This alone does not mean Accepted.
Note from BRENDA
The record shows the source database’s own note under this heading; no paper text is saved.
Several database records
The record merges several database entries; no paper text is saved.
Paper cited (values not located)
A PMID or DOI is linked; where the paper prints the values was not saved.
Database record only
Only the source database record is saved.

Units

kcat
Turnover number, s-1.
Km
Michaelis constant, mM.
kcat/Km
Catalytic efficiency, s-1 mM-1 (multiply by 1,000 for M-1 s-1).
T (°C), pH
Assay temperature and pH; the record lists the other conditions.

On a from-paper record, code converts each printed value to these units (µM to mM, min-1 to s-1). Stored values drop trailing zeros (a printed 0.050 mM is stored as 0.05) and long ones are rounded; the record shows the value as printed beside the stored one.

calc.
A kcat/Km worked out as kcat ÷ Km, not printed in the source; the table and the record show it with the significant figures of the less precise of the two (at least two).
Printed kcat/Km
Kept as printed, also when it differs from kcat ÷ Km; the record then gives both (values differ).
Other units
A few older records keep another unit, shown after the value (min-1, mg/mL), or have no unit; those numbers are not in the column unit.
Large and small
Numbers of 100,000 or more and below 0.001 are written as a × 10n (8.2 × 10-4).
Digits
The table and the record show at most 4 significant figures, and 3 where a conversion from per minute left repeating digits (30.333 shows as 30.3); a whole number keeps all its digits. The tooltip, the record’s values table and the downloads keep the stored digits.
Dash, Unknown
A dash means a value is missing or hidden after a data check. Unknown means not stated in the paper or the source database.

Before you use the values

Review result
Only Accepted records passed review: Not confirmed records have an open question and Not checked records have no review result. Most records have not been checked by a person.
Units
Read the unit beside each value: a few older records keep another unit or none (see Units).
kcat/Km
A printed kcat/Km that differs from kcat ÷ Km is kept and flagged. The record gives both numbers, how far apart they are and, for a familiar factor such as 1000, the likely cause.
Sequences
A variant’s sequence includes its mutations only where CatLog could build it; otherwise the record gives the wild-type reference.
Merged rows
Some older records merge several database rows; the record says how many and lists their papers.
Training sets
Records of one paper share its PMID: split training and test sets by paper or by UniProt accession.

Questions

Why does a value differ from BRENDA?

A from-paper value is read from the paper itself and converted by code, so it can differ from a database entry for the same paper: the database may give it in another unit, round it, or take it from another table, assay condition or enzyme form. The record shows the value as printed and where it is printed. Older records keep their source database’s value.

Why is a sequence or SMILES missing?

CatLog adds a sequence only when it can match the enzyme to a protein entry, and a SMILES only when the substrate name resolves to one structure; otherwise the field stays empty rather than guessed, and the record says None recorded or gives a sequence note. A variant gets its own sequence only where its mutations could be placed; otherwise the record gives the wild-type reference.

What does Not confirmed mean?

The earlier automated review left a question open on the value, unit, protein or substrate, so the record is not accepted. It is replaced when CatLog reads its paper. Use its values with that in mind, or filter to Accepted records.

How do I report an error?

Open the record and choose Report a problem: it starts an e-mail to the lab with the record ID and the snapshot. For anything else, use Report a data issue.

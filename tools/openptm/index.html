Coverage states and the freshness rule

A family counts as covered only when a dedicated or chemically narrow resource with experimental residue-level evidence maps to it. The four states are mutually exclusive across the 111-family denominator:

    “Active, maintained” requires a substantive update on or after 14 August 2021 (a five-year window before the audit date). “No qualifying mapping found” is audit-scoped: it does not establish biological absence, lack of function, or lack of prior study.
